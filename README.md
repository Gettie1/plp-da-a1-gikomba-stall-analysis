# Gikomba Fresh-Produce Stall Sales Analysis

**Author:** Getrude Cheptoo  
**Cohort:** 1 Million Devs Cohort

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 14 days of sales and inventory data from Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market.

Wanjiku has been recording her daily sales manually but needs a better way to understand her business performance, identify sales trends, monitor stock levels, and evaluate product profitability.

The goal of this project is to use core Python programming concepts to analyze the data and generate practical business recommendations that can help Wanjiku make informed decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance over 14 days.
- Compare sales performance between two weeks.
- Classify daily sales as Strong, Normal, or Weak.
- Compare weekday and weekend sales.
- Identify products that need restocking.
- Calculate gross profit margins for each product.
- Generate a formatted business performance report.
- Provide data-driven recommendations.

## Technologies Used

## Key Findings

1. **Weekly sales growth:** Week 2 recorded KES 24,400 compared to KES 22,050 in Week 1, representing a 10.66% increase.

2. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, compared to KES 2,685 on weekdays. Weekend sales averaged 82.50% higher.

3. **Sales classification:** Out of 14 days, 6 were classified as Weak, 4 as Normal, and 4 as Strong.

Additional inventory findings:
- Tomatoes and Sukuma require restocking.
- Avocado recorded the highest gross profit margin at 42.86%.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Increase stock availability for Tomatoes and Sukuma to prevent stock shortages.
- Prepare for higher weekend demand by planning inventory and staffing accordingly.
- Investigate the causes of weak sales days and consider targeted promotions.
- Monitor product profitability and prioritize products with stronger gross margins, such as Avocado.
